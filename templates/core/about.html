About the Church

Our Story

Christ Gospel Evangelical Fire Ministry International is led by a dynamic and anointed man, Pastor Rilwan Olowo Adamu and his amiable wife, Pastor Mrs. Precious Abiodun Rilwan. The ministry is surrounded by a Board of Trustees and dedicated, respected leaders.

Whilst this church is still attaining heights, they have succeeded in spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ far and near. Under three years of establishment — from 2020 till date — with the backing of the Holy Ghost, the Church has been able to plant four daughter churches situated at Ikoya, Akure, Abakaliki and Adamo.

This further proves that their evangelical nature, backed up by Christ Himself, is truly at work for the glory of God.

Symbols of Our Colours

Red — The Blood of the Lamb

The absolute gospel runs red throughout scripture. From Rahab's scarlet cord to Calvary — the blood of Christ that was shed for sinners, interwoven from Genesis to Revelation, cleanses us from all unrighteousness (1 John 1:9).

Blue — The Heavenly Realm

In Ezekiel's vision the throne appeared like sapphire (Ezk 1:26). Blue is symbolic of God's eternal dominion and sovereignty. It will also be a dominant colour in the New Jerusalem that descends out of heaven.

White — Righteousness

"Wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow" (Psalm 51:7). The colour of God's righteousness comes at the expense of the red blood of Christ. Those who wash their robes in the blood of the Lamb are arrayed in white (Rev 7:13).

Gold — God's Love & Glory

The mercy seat was pure gold (Exo 25:17). Gold represents Deity, Divinity, Sovereignty and the refining process. "The tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though tested by fire" (1 Pet 1:7).
